A workshop first. A store second.

Ice Vault opened in {{ site.founded }} as a two-bench shop on the fourth floor of 26 West 47th Street. We still cast, set and polish in that room. The showroom came later.

What we make

Solid gold chains in every link style we can cast. Diamond pendants and rings. Engagement rings built from the setting up. Custom pieces from a client's sketch. We also buy, sell and service watches.

How we work

Every piece is weighed, tested on an XRF machine and photographed before it ships. The weight on the listing is the weight in the box. Diamonds over half a carat come with a GIA or IGI report. Our appraiser signs an insurance appraisal for every order.

We keep prices close to the metal. A chain here costs the gold plus our labor, not a mall markup. If you find the same weight and purity cheaper, call us and we will talk.

The people

Our bench has four jewelers, a setter, a polisher and a watchmaker. Between them they have made jewelry on this street for more than sixty years. The person who answers the phone is a jeweler, not a call center.
