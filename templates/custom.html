Custom jewelry, made with you.

Pendants, rings, chains, bracelets and grillz. You bring the idea. We draw it, price it and make it on 47th Street. Most pieces take 3 to 8 weeks.

  1. Tell us the idea. Fill in the form below, text us photos, or come to the shop. A sketch on a napkin is enough.
  2. Get a render and a price. Within 3 business days we send a 3D render and a fixed quote. Change anything until it is right.
  3. Approve and pay the deposit. 30% starts the work. The balance is due when the piece is finished.
  4. We make it. Casting, setting and polishing happen in our workshop. You get photos at each stage.
  5. Pick it up or we ship it. Insured shipping and a signed appraisal are included.

What it costs

  • Custom pendants start at $1,500 in 10K gold.
  • Custom rings start at $900.
  • Diamond pave adds about $450 per carat in lab diamonds, $1,500 per carat in natural.
  • Rush work in 2 weeks adds 20%.

Materials

  • 10K, 14K, 18K yellow, white and rose gold. Platinum on request.
  • Natural and lab-grown diamonds, VS clarity or better.
  • Emeralds, rubies, sapphires and opals.
  • Hand engraving and enamel.
