Sell your gold, diamonds and watches.

How it works

What we buy

  • Gold in any condition, 10K and up. Broken is fine.
  • Diamonds over 0.5 carats, with or without a report.
  • Rolex, Audemars Piguet, Patek Philippe, Cartier, Omega and Tudor.
  • Platinum and silver by weight.
