Shipping and returns.

Every package is insured for its full value and needs a signature.

Shipping

  • United States: free, insured, 2 to 5 business days by FedEx. Overnight is $45.
  • Canada, UK and EU: $80, insured, 3 to 7 business days. Duties are paid by the buyer.
  • Everywhere else: quoted at checkout. Insured.
  • Stock pieces ship within 2 business days. Sized rings and adjusted bracelets ship within 5. Custom pieces ship when finished.
  • Pick up in the shop is free. We hold pieces for 14 days.

Returns

  • Stock pieces: 30 days from delivery, in new condition with the appraisal and box, for a full refund to the original payment.
  • Sized, engraved, adjusted and custom pieces are final sale.
  • Watches: 7 days, unworn, with all stickers and the warranty card.
  • Email or call for a prepaid, insured return label. Refunds go out within 3 business days of receipt.

Warranty

  • Lifetime warranty on manufacturing defects in every piece we make. We fix loose stones, broken clasps and cracked links at no charge.
  • Watches carry a two-year Ice Vault warranty on the movement.
  • Wear, loss and damage from impact are not covered, but we repair at cost.
